Meatballs Recipie

The standard time to perfect this meatballs recipe is 25 mins. Prep time is approx. 15 and cook time is also 10. Serves 8 people.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 table spoons Italian seasoning
  • 2 teaspoons chopped fresh basil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • ½ cup panko bread crumbs

Steps

    Step 1:
    Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).

    Steps 2:
    Whisk eggs, Italian, seasoning, basil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in a large
    bowl until combined. Add ground beef and panko; mix thoroughly.

    Step 3:
    Shape beef mixture into about 40 golf ball-sized meatballs (about 1 ½-inches).
    Arrange on ungreased, rimmed baking sheets.

    Step 4:
    Bake in a preheated oven until no longer pink in centres, 10 to 15 minutes,
    flipping halfway through. An instant-read thermometer inserted into centres
    should read at least 16 degrees F (70 degrees C).

    Cook´s Note

    You can cook these meatballs in an air fryer if you prefer: Preheat the air fryer
    to 375°F (190°C). Lightly coat the basket with cooking spray. Arrange meatballs
    in a single layer in the basket, leaving space between them. Cook until browned
    and no longer pink in the center, 8 to 10 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through.
